Privacidade Policy

This policy explains clearly how Oolivo handles information when you use the website and its tools.

1. Scope

This Privacidade Policy applies to Oolivo and the tools available at oolivo.com.br. When using the site, you may interact with tools that process text, images, documents, numbers and other data directly in your browser.

2. Data entered into tools

When a tool is designed for local processing, the content or selected file is processed in the user's browser whenever technically possible. This does not mean every feature behaves exactly the same way: some may load libraries, APIs or third-party services required for operation.

Do not submit passwords, banking data, personal documents or confidential information to a tool without first checking how that feature works.

3. Cookies and local storage

The site may use cookies, browser local storage and similar technologies to keep preferences, measure usage, improve the experience, provide features and support advertising. The language selector, for example, may store a language preference in browser storage.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

Oolivo uses or may use Google AdSense to display advertising. Third parties, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this or other websites. Google and its partners may also use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers according to their applicable policies and settings.

Users can manage ad personalization in Google Ads Settings. Information about interest-based advertising is also available at aboutads.info.

5. Google Analytics

Oolivo may use Google Analytics to understand how pages are used, identify problems and improve the platform. Statistical information may include pages visited, events, device, browser, approximate access origin and other technical data made available by the service.

6. Disclosure and third parties

Third-party services may process technical information required for advertising, analytics, libraries or specific features. These services have their own policies and terms. Oolivo does not directly control the privacy practices of external services.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the site and its features available and reduce risks. No Internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid submitting information that is not necessary to perform a task.

8. Children

Oolivo is a general-purpose tools platform and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children to create accounts or profiles.

9. Rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or processing of personal data. Privacidade requests may be sent through the contact channel.

10. Updates

This policy may be updated when there are changes to tools, services, laws or site practices. The version published on this page is the current reference.
